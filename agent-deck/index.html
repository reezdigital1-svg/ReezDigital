      Connect

      What this phone can and cannot do

      • Can: receive jobs from any ring agent; grant scoped one-time reads (pasted text, typed note, one photo, one file) that run only on your tap; auto-expire grants; keep local logs; hard revoke all.
      • Cannot: read your messages, contacts or files unseen. A browser page gets nothing except what you hand it through a tap. That is by design.
      • Next tier: a native app (Android APK) could add share-target automation and background job checks, still behind approve taps.
