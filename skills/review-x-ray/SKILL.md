# Review X-Ray

Marketing written from imagination loses to marketing written from the customer's
own words. This skill extracts the voice of the market from public reviews and
hands it to you as raw material.

## Workflow

1. **Gather sources.** Public reviews, app store listings, Reddit threads, GitHub
   issues, forum complaints. Record the source and date for every quote. Never
   invent a quote; one fabricated review poisons the whole report.
2. **Extract in three buckets:**
   - PRAISE: what users say it does well, in their words.
   - COMPLAINTS: recurring problems, ranked by how often they appear.
   - LANGUAGE: exact phrases users use, the ones a stranger would recognize.
3. **Build the objection ladder.** For each top complaint, write the honest answer
   and whether the product can back it. Claims you cannot back are marked
   UNSUPPORTED and never used in copy.
4. **Positioning line.** One sentence naming who it is for, what pain it removes,
   using the market's own words, not adjectives.
5. **Copy lines.** Five headline options drawn from real phrases. Each line must
   trace to at least one sourced quote.

## Output contract
- SOURCES: list with dates.
- PRAISE / COMPLAINTS: quoted, ranked, counted.
- OBJECTION LADDER: complaint, answer, backed or UNSUPPORTED.
- POSITIONING: one line.
- COPY LINES: five, each with its source quote.

## Rules
- Quotes are receipts: source and date or they do not exist.
- Frequency beats volume: three independent complaints outweigh one angry essay.
- If the source pool is thin, say so and widen it instead of padding.
