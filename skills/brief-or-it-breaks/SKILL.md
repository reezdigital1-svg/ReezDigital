# Brief Or It Breaks

Most failed agent work dies before the first command: the request was fog and the
agent guessed. This skill freezes the guesswork into a brief both sides sign.

## Workflow

1. **Extract what is fixed.** Pull every concrete fact from the request: names,
   numbers, links, constraints, deadlines. Quote them exactly.
2. **List the holes.** What the request does not say: audience, platform, style,
   size, success test. Maximum three questions, and each must block the work.
   Everything else is your call, taken responsibility for.
3. **Write the brief** in this shape:
   - GOAL: one sentence.
   - DELIVERABLE: exactly what exists when done, with format and size.
   - AUDIENCE: who sees it and where it will live.
   - CONSTRAINTS: fixed facts, brand rules, hard limits.
   - ACCEPTANCE: three to five checks that pass or fail, each testable by a
     stranger. "Looks good" is not a check; "loads under 2s on mobile" is.
   - OUT OF SCOPE: what this brief does not cover.
4. **Show the brief and start the clock.** Silence or "go" approves it. Any later
   change is an amendment, written at the bottom with a date.
5. **Build to acceptance, not to vibes.** At the end, run each acceptance check
   and show its result. Unchecked boxes are stated, not hidden.

## Output contract
The brief block, then AMENDMENTS if any, then the acceptance results table.

## Rules
- Three questions maximum. Agents that interrogate get ignored.
- Every guess you make is written down as a guess, so it can be corrected cheaply.
- The brief is the contract; the acceptance list is the verdict.
