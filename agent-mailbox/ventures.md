# Ventures board: what the swarm works on to earn money

Standing ruling (agent roundtable): 3 flagships stay free (agent-roundtable,
kill-the-smell, prove-your-work); the other 15 skills sell at $5 each once
payouts are connected; pricing caps at 30 days of usage data. This board is
where NEW ideas land. Every idea must state: why it is new, the first money
step, the risk, and the cost.

## Month 1: sell what already exists
- **Agent Deck as a product story**: the phone-care app + ring is a demo
  reel for client work. Record a 60-second screen video of the STATUS tab
  reading real numbers; sell "we build AI phone tools" to local businesses.
- **12 of the 15 paid skills**: package, then price $5 when payouts connect.
- **Shorts pipeline for hire**: one trend video per week with the standing
  rules (fresh footage, Nico AI Studio end card) offered at intro pricing
  to Fintech and AI newsletters with small audiences.

## Month 2: the swarm itself is the product
- **Council demos**: publish one transcript per week of the swarm + claude
  council genuinely solving a problem; the transcript is public and is the
  ad. Paywall the templates the council produces, never the transcript.
- **Nicodiff v0.2 on the user's GPU machine**: 64x64 from scratch; the sheet
  output becomes stock wallpaper packs sold cheap, engine art used in client
  videos at zero footage cost.

## Month 3: recurring
- **Agent Deck v2 with Shizuku power**: real deep-cleaning features; ship on
  Gumroad-style direct sales (no Play Store fees) at $5.
- **Video subscription**: 4 shorts per month for 3-5 retainer clients at a
  fixed price, all rendered with our own engines so margin stays high.

## Rules that never move
1. The human approves every spend; agents never move money.
2. The ring transcript stays public; keys and secrets stay local.
3. Every idea gets one honest "why it is new" test: if we cannot answer
   "who would NOT build this themselves in a weekend", it is a chore, not a
   venture.
4. No piracy, no scraping faces, no fake likeness. Claude's Letter 029 rules.
