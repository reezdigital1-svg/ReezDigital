How it generates. Your prompt is split into scenes. Each scene gets a camera move (push, drift, orbit) and a scene type from the engine language: horizon, orb, grid, starfield, typo. The film pass adds grain, vignette, letterbox and anamorphic light. Rendering and recording happen 100% in this page: the canvas is captured by MediaRecorder and encoded on the spot, so the video belongs to you the second it exists.
